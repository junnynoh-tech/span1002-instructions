🖥️ Lab 2 Video Activity — Rutina saludable
🎬Video Assignment — Health Ambassador Presentation

You have been selected as a Health Ambassador for UNG's incoming freshman class. Create a slide presentation and record yourself in Bongo using Camera and Screen, walking new students through the different places and moments in your day where you practice healthy habits. Your goal is to inspire them with a realistic, personal routine.

🖼️What a good slide looks like
📷 Slide Example — El cuarto de baño
El cuarto de baño
lavarse los dientes ducharse peinarse
"Me lavo los dientes dos veces al día — nunca me olvido porque me molesta no hacerlo. También me ducho por la mañana y me peino antes de salir. A mi compañero de cuarto le encanta ducharse de noche."
🔊
✅ Good slide

El gimnasio
ejercitarse · estirarse · sudar

Keywords only — you speak the full sentences live

❌ Not this

"En el gimnasio me ejercito todos los días porque es muy importante para la salud."

Full sentences on slides = you are reading, not presenting

🖼️Feel free to include images on your slides — pictures make the presentation more engaging and give your audience visual context. Keywords and images only; full sentences are spoken.

💡 Suggested places: el dormitorio (por la mañana), el baño, la cafetería, el comedor, la biblioteca / sala de estudio, el exterior (caminando entre clases), el dormitorio (por la noche). You can substitute — the places must be real parts of your day.
🗣️What to say for each place — go beyond the basics

For each slide, cover at least three of the following — vary what you say so it doesn't sound repetitive:

  • 1
    Los verbos reflexivos: Use at least 5 different Ch. 7 reflexive verbs correctly: despertarse, levantarse, ducharse, bañarse, lavarse, cepillarse, peinarse, maquillarse, afeitarse, vestirse, acostarse, dormirse.
  • 2
    Los negativos e indefinidos: Include at least one negative or indefinite expression from Ch. 7: nunca, siempre, a veces, nadie, nada, también.
  • 3
    Los verbos como gustar: Use at least one gustar-type verb to explain a habit: me encanta, me molesta, me importa, me gusta, me fascina…
  • 4
    ¿Por qué?: For each habit, give a reason — not just what you do, but why it matters for staying healthy.
💡 Your video should sound like a genuine health presentation — varied, specific, and personal. Avoid repeating the same structure for every slide.

🎬 Alternative Recording Methods
Use these only if Bongo Camera and Screen fails
Option A — PowerPoint with Camera (UNG Microsoft 365)

Free with your UNG account — sign in at office.com with your UNG email.

1
Open PowerPoint and build your slides
2
Go to Slide Show → Record Slide Show — enable your camera in the top toolbar
3
Present through each slide, then click Stop
4
Export: File → Export → Create a Video → save as MP4
5
Open the Bongo assignment → click Upload (not Record) → select your MP4
Option B — Canva Present and Record

Free account at canva.com — no UNG login needed.

1
Build your slides at canva.com
2
Click Present → Present and Record — your face appears in the corner
3
Speak through each slide, then click End Recording
4
Download as MP4 → open the Bongo assignment → click Upload → select your file
💡Both options produce a video file you upload through Bongo. Your face must be visible throughout and your audio must be clear.
