Key Points to Remember

  • 🕐No late submissions. Contact the Language Lab before the deadline if you have a technology issue — not after.
  • 🖥️Camera and Screen mode (Labs 2 & 6). Open your slides in a separate window before starting Bongo. Share the whole window, not a browser tab.
  • 📱Lab 3 is a phone video. Record on your phone, transfer to your computer, then upload through Bongo — do not use the Record button.
  • 📖Approved resources only. Class notes, your Vistas textbook, and a dictionary. Do not read from a prepared script.
  • 📊Rubric available in Bongo. Open the assignment to see the grading rubric before you record.
  • 🚫Academic integrity. Online translators, AI tools, or outside help may result in serious academic consequences.
  • 📬Check your UNG email. If due dates or instructions change, you will be notified there.

Grading

Lab assignments are graded by Language Lab staff — not your course instructor. Grading typically takes about one week. Questions about grades go directly to the Language Lab — not your professor.

🏅 Up to 25 points per lab for completion
