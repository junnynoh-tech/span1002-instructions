🎥 Lab 5 — La salud física y mental
🎬How to complete this activity

Open the Bongo assignment for this lab. Watch each question video and record your response. Speak only in Spanish using complete sentences.

📌 This lab has 5 separate video prompts. Respond to each one individually and completely before moving to the next.
⚠️ Detail is important for this lab. Give specific, realistic recommendations — use debes, tienes que, and es importante with infinitives. Use the preterite for your personal past experiences in prompt 5.
✅Before you submit — check your recording
  • 1
    Play back your full recording before submitting
  • 2
    Audio test: Can you hear your voice clearly throughout? No audio = no credit.
  • 3
    Face check: Is your face visible the entire time? No face = no credit.
  • 4
    Did you give complete, detailed answers?
