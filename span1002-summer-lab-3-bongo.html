▶️How to submit your cooking video
  1. 1Record your cooking video in Spanish on your phone. Cover all four parts: introducción, ingredientes, pasos, ¡listo!
  2. 2Transfer the video to your computer.

    📱 iPhone → Mac

    AirDrop is fastest. Or save to iCloud Drive.

    🤖 Android → PC

    USB cable or upload to Google Drive and download.

    📧 Any phone

    Email the file to yourself and download it.

    ☁️ Any phone

    Google Drive, Dropbox, or OneDrive — upload then download.

  3. 3Open the Lab 3 Bongo assignment in D2L.
  4. 4Click Upload — not Record. Select your video file and wait for it to finish uploading.
  5. 5Preview the video in Bongo to confirm it plays correctly, then click Submit.
💡Any dish is fine — ramen, a sandwich, scrambled eggs, anything you can prepare. Supported formats: MP4, MOV, AVI. If the file is too large, trim it or lower the resolution before uploading.
✅Before you submit
  • Played back the full video
  • Face is visible the entire time
  • Audio is clear — you can hear your voice throughout
  • Spoke only in Spanish throughout
  • Covered all four required parts
  • Recording is the right length — 1.5–2.5 minutes
⋮
Uploaded the wrong file? Click the three-dot menu (⋮) at the top-right of the Bongo page → Reset Submission. Available any time before the deadline.
🔧 Having trouble? Use Google Chrome on a laptop. Clear cache and cookies if the recorder won't load. Contact the Language Lab before the deadline if you have a technical issue.
