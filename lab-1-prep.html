Where…?

Ask at least three follow-up questions.

📘 Saber vs. Conocer

Saber

Sé cuánto cuesta la camisa.

I know how much the shirt costs.

Conocer

Conozco la tienda en el centro.

I know the store downtown.

Try to include one sentence with saber and one sentence with conocer.

🎥 Observe & Describe: Use the preterite

There is an Observe & Describe activity at the end of each lab, which consists in watching a video or observing an image, and following the prompts. In this lab, after watching the video, describe what happened using the preterite tense.

Aim for 8–9 short phrases. These should describe completed actions or events you saw in the video.

What does this look like?

Examples of short phrases:

  • Entró en la tienda.
  • Miró la ropa.
  • Compró una camisa.
  • Pagó con tarjeta.

Speak naturally. Do not write anything down.
