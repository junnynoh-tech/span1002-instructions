▶️How to record in Bongo
  1. 1Open the Lab 2 Bongo assignment in D2L. Click the link to launch Bongo.
  2. 2Allow camera, microphone, and screen sharing when Chrome asks.
  3. 3Select Camera and Screen mode — not Camera only. This captures both your face and your slides.
  4. 4Have your slides open in a separate window before you click Record. Share that window when prompted.
  5. 5Click Record. Advance slides manually as you speak. Keep your face in the camera overlay.
  6. 6Click Stop when finished. Play back your recording before submitting.
  7. 7Click Submit when satisfied.
💡Prepare your slides in PowerPoint, Google Slides, or Canva before opening Bongo. Keywords only on slides — full sentences are spoken, not read. If Camera and Screen fails, use the Alternative Recording option in the lab instructions.
✅Before you submit
  • Played back the full recording
  • Face is visible the entire time
  • Audio is clear — you can hear your voice throughout
  • Spoke only in Spanish throughout
  • Recording is 2–3 minutes in length
⋮
Need to re-record? Click the three-dot menu (⋮) at the top-right of the Bongo page → Reset Submission. Available any time before the deadline.
🔧 Having trouble? Use Google Chrome on a laptop. Clear cache and cookies if the recorder won't load. For Camera and Screen, close other apps using the mic or camera first. Contact the Language Lab before the deadline if you have a technical issue.
